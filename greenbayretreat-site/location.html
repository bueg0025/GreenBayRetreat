Location

Everything worth doing, a short walk or drive away

Green Bay Retreat sits about 1 mile from Lambeau Field off Oneida St. — close enough to walk to the stadium and Titletown District, with downtown Green Bay's food and drink scene just minutes further.

Landmarks

Attractions

  • Lambeau Field

    Home of the Green Bay Packers, with stadium tours available. About 1 mile from the house.

  • Titletown District

    Outdoor activities, restaurants, and entertainment right next to Lambeau Field.

  • National Railroad Museum

    Family-friendly and educational — a great rainy-day option.

  • Bay Beach Amusement Park

    Classic rides and fun for all ages, with famously affordable ticket pricing.

Outdoors

Outdoor activities

  • Bay Beach Wildlife Sanctuary

    Walking trails, wildlife, and a nature center.

  • Fox River Trail

    A scenic walking and biking path along the river.

Coffee

Coffee & cafe spots

  • Kavarna Coffeehouse

    Located in the Broadway district — espresso bar, house-made bakery, and sandwiches/pitas/wraps/salads made with local ingredients.

  • The Attic

    Espresso bar, extensive tea selection, and a used book store in one — a relaxed, artsy cafe.

  • Bhava Coffee

    Great for coffee and light breakfast/pastry options, with a drive-thru.

  • Glas Coffee

    A strong local cafe with a nice ambiance.

  • Coffee Wizardz

    A wizard-themed coffee shop with fresh-roasted beans and creative latte drinks.

  • Leaps and Bounds Cafe

    Smoothies, coffee, wine, and cocktails in a casual, elegant Kohler Hotel cafe.

  • The Daily Buzz

    A modern coffeehouse for espresso drinks and teas, plus bagels, sandwiches, and baked goods.

Morning

Breakfast & brunch

  • The Pancake Place

    Classic diner-style comfort food, with breakfast served all day and pancakes as the main draw.

  • Bay Family Restaurant

    Home-style cooking, open for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

  • Delilah's

    Downtown brunch spot with Southern touches like grits and biscuits & gravy.

  • The Creamery Downtown

    Brunch and locally-roasted coffee, with creative twists on breakfast classics.

  • Grapevine Cafe

    A casual neighborhood cafe with an attached gift shop — friendly local spot for breakfast and brunch.

  • Golden Basket Restaurant

    Comfort breakfast with solid all-day options.

  • Allouez Cafe

    An old-fashioned standby serving home-style American meals for breakfast and lunch.

Pizza

Pizza spots

  • Rustique Pizzeria + Lounge

    Housed in a 122-year-old chapel, known for thin New York-style pizza, creative toppings, and a lounge atmosphere. Dine-in or pick-up.

  • Sammy's Pizza & Italian Restaurant

    A longtime family place — dough, sauce, and sausage from family recipes.

  • Glass Nickel Pizza Co.

    A local chain known for specialty pizzas in thin or hand-tossed styles, dine-in or delivery.

  • Gallagher's Pizza

    Classic and gourmet pizzas plus a pizza buffet with scratch-made dough and locally-sourced ingredients.

  • Parker John's BBQ + Pizza

    A combo of smoked BBQ and stone-fired pizza.

  • Jake's Pizza

    A local, community spot with fresh daily dough, sauces, and sausage — plus board and video games.

  • Cranky Pat's Pizza Parlor

    A classic neighborhood joint with thin crust pizza, an intimate bar, and live music.

Craft Beer

Breweries

  • Titletown Brewing Company

    One of Green Bay's original craft breweries, in a restored cannery — house brews and pub grub.

  • Badger State Brewing Company

    Rotating selection with a strong local vibe.

  • Hinterland Brewery & Restaurant

    Nice ambiance with good food-pairing options and a wide rotating beer list.

  • Stillmank Brewing Company

    A smaller-scale, friendly taproom with good variety.

  • Noble Roots Brewing Company

    A good taproom vibe with both approachable and adventurous styles.

  • Copper State Brewing Company

    Solid taproom with a broad selection of styles, good for food pairing.

Game Day

Bars for Packers game day

  • Stadium View Sports Bar~1 block from Lambeau

    A huge multi-bar setup — sports bar, beer garden, and rooftop overlooking the stadium.

  • The Bar — Holmgren Way~1 block from Lambeau

    The official Packers bar HQ, with tailgate packages and walking distance to the stadium.

  • D2 Sports PubNext to the Resch Center

    Marketed as the closest bar to Lambeau Field — classic sports bar with great game-day energy.

  • Anduzzi's Sports Club~1 block from Lambeau

    A big sports bar vibe with lots of TVs, great for crowds pre/post game.

  • 1919 Kitchen + TapInside Lambeau Field

    Over 40 craft beers with a strong food and bar atmosphere.

  • Hinterland BreweryTitletown District

    Great for combining food and beer before or after the game.

Getting Around

Transportation

  • Green Bay Austin Straubel International Airport (GRB)

    About 5 miles away — roughly an 11-minute drive.

  • Green Bay Metro

    The local public transit bus system.

  • Rideshare

    Uber and Lyft are both readily available.
